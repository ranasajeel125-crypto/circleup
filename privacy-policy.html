Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

CircleUp ("we", "us", "our") operates the community discussion website at circleup-ebkw.onrender.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

CircleUp does not require you to create an account or sign in to browse, read, or vote on posts. We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details to use the site.

The only personal information we may receive is information you choose to send us yourself — for example, if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use that information only to reply to you, and we do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and pages requested) for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to identify individual visitors.

2. Cookies and local storage

CircleUp itself does not set cookies and does not store data in your browser. Any cookies on this site come from the third-party advertising partners described below.

3. Third-party advertising

We show ads on this website that are served by third-party advertising vendors, including Google and Monetag.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Other advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads and to measure their performance.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices. Blocking cookies in your browser settings will also stop most advertising cookies from being set.

4. Analytics

We do not run analytics tools that collect personal information about you. If we add an analytics tool in the future, this policy will be updated first.

5. Children's privacy

CircleUp is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us at the email address below and we will delete it.

6. Links to other websites

Posts on CircleUp may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any website you visit.

7. How long we keep information

Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer your message and deal with the matter you raised, and are then deleted.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add a new feature or advertising partner. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change when we do, and the new version applies from the date it is published here.

9. Contact us
