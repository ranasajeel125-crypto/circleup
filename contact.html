What to write about

  • Questions — anything about CircleUp, its communities, or how the site works.
  • Feedback — ideas, suggestions, or things you think we should improve.
  • Corrections — if you spot a mistake in a post or a page, tell us and we will look into it.
  • Content issues — if you believe a post breaks our rules, is misleading, or infringes your rights, please send us the link to the post and a short description of the problem. We review every report.

Please include the link to the relevant page or post when you write to us — it helps us find the issue faster. We read every email and reply as soon as we can.

For privacy questions, see our Privacy Policy. To learn more about the site, see the About page.
