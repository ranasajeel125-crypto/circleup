About CircleUp

Last updated: 13 November 2026

CircleUp is a community discussion platform. People join communities built around the topics they care about, post questions and ideas, vote on the posts they like, and discuss them with other members.

The idea is simple: good discussions rise to the top. A thoughtful question, a useful answer, or an interesting take gets voted up, so the best of each community is easy to find.

Our communities

CircleUp has ten communities:

  • Technology — news, reviews and discussion about the technology shaping our lives: phones, AI, software, startups and the internet.
  • WorldNews — major news from around the world. Stories should link to a credible source, and headlines should match the article.
  • Gaming — games, consoles, esports and everything players love: reviews, news and honest discussion.
  • Movies — from Hollywood blockbusters to hidden indie gems: reviews, recommendations and film discussion.
  • Science — research and science news, explained clearly. Speculation is fine — label it as such.
  • Foodies — recipes, restaurant finds, street food and home cooking from every corner of the world.
  • Travel — trip reports, hidden places, travel tips and photography from travellers everywhere.
  • PersonalFinance — budgeting, saving, investing basics and money questions, explained in plain language.
  • Cricket — match threads, analysis and debate for cricket fans. All formats, all teams, one community.
  • AskAnything — ask the community anything. The best answers rise to the top; curiosity is the only requirement.

How it works

Browse the home feed to see what communities are talking about, or open a community to see only its posts. Join a community to follow it, post a question or discussion of your own, and vote on posts and comments. Every community has its own rules in its description — please read them before posting.

Be kind

CircleUp works best when discussions stay respectful. No harassment, hate speech, spam, or misleading content. If you see a post that breaks the rules, please let us know through the Contact page.
